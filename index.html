What price levels are doing now

Simple explanation

Level test = price came back to an important area.

Rejection = price touched an area and candles moved away from it.

Hold = price stayed around the area instead of clearly leaving it.

Pullback = price moved away and then came back toward the area.

Repeated test = the same area was visited multiple times.

Expansion after test = candle ranges became larger after an interaction.

How to read the dashboard

1. Start with "What is happening now?"

Look for whether candles are becoming larger/smaller, changing direction, testing areas, or showing rejection.

2. Read the story left → right

Do not focus on one word. The order matters. A story is a sequence of candle behaviours.

3. Compare timeframes

Short timeframes describe faster behaviour. Longer timeframes describe the broader candle structure around the same historical point.

4. Use history as examples

Historical matches show what similar structures looked like before. They do not tell us what must happen next.
